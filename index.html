Claudiatron

Role: Game Designer Team: 2 Unity 4 weeks May 2026

A taxi game about keeping a teleporting passenger from escaping while you drive, eat, and smoke. A door-handle sound tells you when she’s trying to get out.

My work
Control tuning · Pickup route guidance · Seated passenger pose

Making the driving feel controllable

The controls felt uncontrollable beyond the chaos we intended, and the car sometimes floated. I helped refine control sensitivity and braking responsiveness. The goal was to keep the player in control while preserving the chaotic driving experience.

Environment contribution
Applied the provided textures to an existing town model.

Developed with Caden Stribling.

Nocturne

Role: Game Developer Unreal Engine · Blueprints Windows

My contribution
Enemy AI · Core gameplay systems · Playtesting

Explore a dark house to collect sheet music, find keys to new rooms, and keep your flashlight supplied with batteries. A guitar fires bubbles that slow pursuing enemies, giving you a chance to get away.

Enemy behavior

I created enemy behavior that chases and attacks the player, including attack animations. When the player moves out of view, the enemy returns to patrol. The behavior tree is shown in the breakdown.

Mechanics and navigation

The breakdown connects the guitar’s slowing effect and flashlight switching to their Blueprints. An annotated map and route diagram show how the rooms, keys, flashlight, and guitar fit into the player’s progress.

Playtesting
The full PDF also documents an informal playtest with friends, with photos of the session.
